Straight answers

How does billing work?

Stay Visible and Keep Growing are billed monthly through Stripe. The Social Kit is a one-time payment. In Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt you pay in riyals, dirhams or Egyptian pounds with VAT added to the price shown; anywhere else in US dollars, with no VAT. Brand Deck is coming soon. Orders and payments are not open yet.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time before your next renewal by emailing team@crierstudio.com. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you have already paid for, so you keep your plan until then.

Is The Social Kit included in the monthly plans?

Yes. Stay Visible and Keep Growing both include The Social Kit in your first month. Its 10 social designs count toward your first month's designs.

Can I buy The Social Kit on its own?

Yes. The Social Kit is a one-time payment, for one business, with no subscription.

What is the Store Kit?

The Store Kit is a set of pieces your store uses every day, such as aprons, menu boards, door mats, cups, QR stands and stamp cards, designed for your business in your own identity. It is not open yet. The In-store page shows the kind of pieces a kit for a café, a restaurant or a salon could include.

When does the Store Kit launch?

There is no launch date yet. Join the waitlist and we will email you when it opens.

Can I order custom pieces now?

Yes. Create a free account in the client portal (the Request a quote button on the In-store page) and tell us there what you want to make, where it will be used and your timing.

Who makes the pieces?

Our own workshop makes them, and we check every piece.

What is a Brand Deck?

Brand Deck is coming soon. Orders and payments are not open yet. A brand deck made from your website, with your logo. It has an identity page, one page per product and a catalogue, and comes as a screen PDF and a print-ready PDF file.

Is my logo changed?

No. Brand Deck is not a logo redesign. We use your existing logo as it is.

What files do I get?

A screen PDF (A4 landscape, RGB) and a print-ready PDF file (CMYK, 3 mm bleed, crop marks). They are digital files only; nothing physical is shipped.

What does the Brand Deck cost?

Brand Deck is coming soon. Orders and payments are not open yet.

How long does it take?

We do not promise a delivery time up front. We confirm the timing by email when we reply.

Can you print it?

We give you a print-ready PDF file. Printing itself is not included, and nothing physical is shipped.

What do you need from me?

For The Social Kit or a monthly plan: your business name, website or Instagram, email, city, country and industry; then you pay securely on Stripe. The Brand Deck is coming soon and cannot be ordered yet.

It helps if you send your logo, your brand colors if you have them, and your own photos. Everything is built around your existing logo and approved assets, and we don't use stock photos of strangers in your posts.

Which languages do you work in?

This site is in English and Arabic, and you can write to us in either. Your posts and captions can be in Arabic, English or both. A Brand Deck can be made in English or Arabic, or in both together.

Who publishes the posts?

You do. The designs, captions and plans are files ready for you or your team to publish. Nothing is published for you.

Who owns what you make for me?

The deck files, designs and captions we deliver to you are yours to use in your business. Your logo, name and brand stay yours, and we do not show work made for you in public without your written OK.

Can you promise more customers?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that. We deliver the kit, the designs and the plans described on the pricing page. Results depend on your business, your market and what you do with them.

What is the client portal?

A private page for clients at portal.crierstudio.com, where we share your work and you can review it. Accounts for our packages are opened after you pay; to tell us about custom pieces, you can create an account there yourself.

What happens to my details?

We use them to prepare what you asked for and email you about it. You can ask us to export or delete them any time at team@crierstudio.com. See the privacy policy for the details.

Is Crier Studio a registered company?

Not yet. Contact is by email only: team@crierstudio.com.
