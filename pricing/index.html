How paying works

  • You pay securely on Stripe. Work starts once it is paid.
  • In Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt you pay in riyals, dirhams or Egyptian pounds, with VAT (15%, 5% or 14%) added. You see the price, the VAT and the total before you pay. Anywhere else you pay in US dollars, with no VAT.
  • The Social Kit is a one-time payment. Monthly plans renew each month: cancel any time before renewal and keep your plan until the paid month ends.
  • Not happy with what we deliver? Email us within 7 days of your purchase and we will agree a fix or a refund with you in writing.
