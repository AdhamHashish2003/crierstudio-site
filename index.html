Straight answers

How does billing work?

You pay securely on Stripe from your client portal. Monthly plans are billed each month; the one-time package is paid once. In Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt you pay in riyals, dirhams or Egyptian pounds with VAT added to the price shown; anywhere else in US dollars, with no VAT. The pricing page lists what each package includes.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time before your next renewal by emailing team@crierstudio.com. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you have already paid for, so you keep your plan until then.

Do the monthly plans include the starter package?

Yes. Both monthly plans include the one-time starter package in your first month. Its 10 social designs count toward your first month's designs.

Can I pay once, with no subscription?

Yes. The starter package is a one-time payment, for one business, with no subscription.

What is coming to In-store?

A set of pieces your store uses every day, such as aprons, menu boards, door mats, cups, QR stands and stamp cards, designed for your business in your own identity. It is not open yet. The In-store page shows the kind of pieces a café, a restaurant or a salon could get.

When does In-store open?

There is no launch date yet. Join the waitlist and we will email you when it opens.

Can I order custom pieces now?

Yes. Create a free account in the client portal (the Request a quote button on the In-store page) and tell us there what you want to make, where it will be used and your timing.

Who makes the pieces?

Our own workshop makes them, and we check every piece.

What do you need from me?

Create your account in the client portal and tell us about your business: its name, your website or Instagram, your city, country and industry. You choose your package and pay securely on Stripe from your portal.

It helps if you send your logo, your brand colors if you have them, and your own photos. Everything is built around your existing logo and approved assets.

Which languages do you work in?

This site is in English and Arabic, and you can write to us in either. Your posts and captions can be in Arabic, English or both. A Brand Deck can be made in English or Arabic, or in both together.

Who publishes the posts?

You do. The designs, captions and plans are files ready for you or your team to publish. Nothing is published for you.

Who owns what you make for me?

The deck files, designs and captions we deliver to you are yours to use in your business. Your logo, name and brand stay yours, and we do not show work made for you in public without your written OK.

Can you promise more customers?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that. We deliver the designs and the plans described on the pricing page. Results depend on your business, your market and what you do with them.

What is the client portal?

A private page for clients at portal.crierstudio.com, where we share your work and you review and approve it. You can create your account there yourself.

What happens to my details?

We use them to prepare what you asked for and email you about it. You can ask us to export or delete them any time at team@crierstudio.com. See the privacy policy for the details.

Is Crier Studio a registered company?

Not yet. Contact is by email only: team@crierstudio.com.
