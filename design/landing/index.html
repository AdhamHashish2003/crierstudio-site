Three landing pages

Design versions of the crierstudio.com home page. They use the live site's palette, fonts, copy and prices. This folder is excluded from GitHub Pages (_config.yml), so nothing here is public.

Version A · darkBillboard

Poster-scale type, a lime highlight on the headline, a moving strip of example posts, a full-bleed lime band for the OK rule, and pricing as one comparison table.

Open A → Version B · lightClient page

Product-led, on the site's light tokens. An example Instagram profile with an approval card in the hero, and a clickable client page (Overview, Calendar, Review queue) you can approve a post in.

Open B → Version C · darkSpec sheet

Split screen: the pitch and CTA stay fixed on the left with a live section index; the right side scrolls through ruled panels covering the kit, the steps, pricing rows and In-store.
