Straight answers

Questions? Email team@crierstudio.com, or read the full FAQ.

How does billing work?

You pay securely on Stripe from your client portal. Monthly plans are billed each month; the one-time package is paid once. Checkout is in US dollars.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time before your next renewal by emailing team@crierstudio.com. You keep your plan until the end of the month you have paid for.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, your website or Instagram, your city and country, what you sell, who your customers are, your offers and the style you like. If you have them, your logo, brand colours and your own photos.

Who publishes the posts?

You do. The designs, captions and plans are files ready for you or your team to publish. Nothing is published for you.

Who owns what you make for me?

The files, designs and captions we deliver are yours to use in your business. We do not show work made for you in public without your written OK.

Can you promise more customers?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that. Results depend on your business, your market and what you do with the posts.
