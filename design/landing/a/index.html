Straight answers

More on the FAQ page, or email team@crierstudio.com.

How does billing work?

You pay securely on Stripe from your client portal. Monthly plans are billed each month; the one-time package is paid once. Checkout is in US dollars.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time before your next renewal by emailing team@crierstudio.com. You keep your plan until the end of the month you have paid for.

Can I pay once, with no subscription?

Yes. The Social Kit is a one-time payment, for one business, with no subscription.

Who publishes the posts?

You do. The designs, captions and plans are files ready for you or your team to publish. Nothing is published for you.

Which languages do you work in?

Your posts and captions can be in Arabic, English or both. You can write to us in either.

Can you promise more customers?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that. We deliver the designs and the plans described on the pricing page. Results depend on your business, your market and what you do with them.
