Common questions

Is there a contract or a minimum period?

No. You pay one month at a time by InstaPay, and nothing is charged automatically.

Who publishes the posts?

We do. After you approve each post, we publish it on your pages and reply to comments and messages.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, some photos of your business and products, and answers to a few questions about your business.

Which platforms do you cover?

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, in every package.

Which languages do you write in?

Arabic, English or both, as you prefer.

What if I'm not happy?

Ask for a change: every design in your package has revision rounds. We do not give refunds for a paid month, and you can cancel any time before your next month starts. See the refund policy.
