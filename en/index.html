Common questions

Who publishes the posts?

We do. After you approve each post, we publish it on your pages and reply to comments and messages.

How do I subscribe and pay?

Tap Subscribe on WhatsApp under the package you want. We send you the InstaPay details; you pay, send the receipt, and we start. Or create your account and tell us about your business first, then message us on WhatsApp to pay.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, some photos of your business and products, and answers to a few questions about your business.

Do I see the posts before they go live?

Yes. Every post waits for your approval: approve it or ask for a change.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Packages are paid monthly and nothing is charged automatically. Tell us before your next month starts.
