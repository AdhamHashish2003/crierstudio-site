Terms of service

The terms for using crierstudio.com, the monthly packages and the client portal. Last updated 18 November 2026.

Who we are

Crier Studio is a design studio for local businesses. Contact us by email at team@crierstudio.com or on WhatsApp at +20 114 600 0101.

What we offer

Monthly packages, for one business each, billed monthly: Bronze (EGP 2,500 a month): 4 social designs with captions, a story with every post and 2 reels a month, a monthly content calendar, 3 platforms and 1 revision round per piece. Silver (EGP 3,500 a month): 7 social designs with captions, a story with every post and 3 reels a month, a monthly content calendar, 3 platforms and 1 revision round per piece. Gold (EGP 5,000 a month): 12 social designs with captions, a story with every post and 5 reels a month, a monthly content calendar, 3 platforms and 2 revision rounds per piece. Diamond (EGP 6,500 a month): 15 social designs with captions, a story with every post and 8 reels a month, a monthly content calendar, 3 platforms and 4 revision rounds per piece. Every package includes all three platforms (Instagram, Facebook and TikTok); we publish on your pages and reply to comments and messages; and your brand look (colours, fonts, post templates) is set up in your first month and used on every post. The work is made from what you tell us about your business, using your existing logo and the materials you approve.

Prices: for now we work with businesses in Egypt. The prices above are in Egyptian pounds, and the price shown is the amount due. What each package includes is listed on the pricing page.

Payment and billing

You pay in Egyptian pounds: for now we work with businesses in Egypt. The price shown is the amount due. You subscribe to a package on WhatsApp; we send you our InstaPay details, you pay by InstaPay and send us the receipt, and work starts once the payment has arrived. You can also create your account and tell us about your business first, then pay the same way. Nothing is charged without your agreement. You also use a client portal account to give us your business details and approve your posts.

Packages are paid monthly by InstaPay. Nothing is charged automatically: each month starts when that month's payment has arrived. To stop, tell us on WhatsApp or by email at team@crierstudio.com, or use the Cancel button in your client portal, before your next month starts; you keep the package until the end of the month you have paid for.

Your logo and materials

You give us permission to use your logo, name, photos and public information only to make the work in your package. You confirm that you have the right to give this permission. We keep your logo as it is and do not redesign it.

Client portal account

You create your client portal account yourself at portal.crierstudio.com. It is for your business only. Keep your sign-in details private and tell us at once if you think someone else has used them. You can ask us to close the account at any time.

No promised results

We do not promise more customers, more sales, a search ranking or any other result. What we deliver is what is described on the pricing page. What happens next depends on your business, your market and what you do with it.

What we need from you

Accurate details, and only photos, logos and text you have the right to use. Nothing is published on your pages before you approve it.

Publishing and replies

Every package includes publishing and replies. For this you give us access to your Instagram, Facebook and TikTok pages, and you can remove that access at any time. We publish only posts you have approved. Our team reads and handles the comments and messages on your pages. Questions about health, complaints or refunds are passed to you to answer.

Public sources

We review your site and profiles and, for comparisons, public information about similar businesses. If we cannot find comparable businesses we say so. Numbers come from public sources and can be incomplete or out of date.

Ownership of what we make for you

The posts, captions and other files we deliver to you are yours to use in your business. Your logo, name and brand stay yours. The examples on this site are made for fictional businesses.

Refunds

Monthly packages are not refunded once a month has been paid. You can cancel at any time before your next month starts, and nothing renews automatically. If you are not happy with a design, ask for a change within your package's revision rounds. See the refund policy.

Limits

We provide our services as described on this site. We do our best to be accurate, but public information we rely on can be incomplete or out of date, so check each post before you approve it. Questions about these terms: team@crierstudio.com.

Changes

We may update these terms, and the date above will change when we do. An update does not change what we have already agreed with you in writing.
