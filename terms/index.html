Terms of service

The terms for using crierstudio.com, The Social Kit, the monthly plans, the Brand Deck and the client portal. Last updated 14 October 2026.

Who we are

Crier Studio is not yet a registered company. Contact is by email only: team@crierstudio.com.

What we offer

The Social Kit ($29, one-time, for one business): a complete marketing + brand-book PDF; a business review and 3 competitor profiles; positioning, audience hypotheses and key messages; logo usage, colors, type and voice guidelines; 10 social designs with captions; 5 stories; 1 campaign concept with 3 ad-copy variations; the relevant standard brand mockups (up to 11); website/SEO priorities and a 30-day plan; and the individual creative files, with 1 correction round. It is built around your existing logo and approved assets.

Brand Deck (coming soon, not on sale yet; planned one-time price $250): a brand deck made from your public pages, with your existing logo, showing up to 11 of your products, delivered as digital files only: a screen PDF and a print-ready PDF file, with 1 correction round. Nothing physical is printed or shipped. Orders and payments are not open yet.

Monthly plans: Stay Visible ($45 a month) and Keep Growing ($60 a month). Both include The Social Kit in your first month. What each includes is listed on the pricing page.

Payment and billing

Buyers in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt are charged in Saudi riyals, UAE dirhams or Egyptian pounds: the price shown plus VAT at 15%, 5% or 14%, shown as price + VAT = total before you pay. Buyers in any other country are charged the US-dollar price, with no VAT added. You pay securely on Stripe when you order, and work starts once it is paid. Client portal accounts are opened after payment.

Monthly plans are billed monthly. You can cancel any time before your next renewal by emailing team@crierstudio.com; cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you have paid for.

The Social Kit is included in your first month of Stay Visible or Keep Growing, and its 10 social designs count toward that month's designs.
