## Section outline

1. **Hero.** H1 and one line: «بعد موافقتك على المنشورات، نصنع لكل منشور ستوري من 3 إلى 5 إطارات نصية متحركة.» / "Once you approve
   your posts, we make a story for each one: 3 to 5 animated text frames."
2. **What a story is.** Short animated text frames that carry the post's message into Stories on Instagram and Facebook
   (and TikTok where it fits), in the brand look (colours, fonts) from month 1.
3. **Made after approved posts.** Stories follow the posts you approved, so they say the same thing; you can still ask for a
   change before they go live. Nothing is published before you approve it.
4. **Published for you.** A person on our team publishes them and replies to the messages they bring.
5. **Part of every package.** "A story with every post" is in every package (`pkg.*.f2`); link to /pricing/.
6. **Close.** Subscribe on WhatsApp / create your account.
