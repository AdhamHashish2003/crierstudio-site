## Section outline

1. **Hero.** H1 and one line: «تخبرنا بما تريد أن يقوله الريل وترسل حتى 6 صور، ونصنع منها ريل قصيرًا لمشروعك.» / "Tell us what the
   reel should say and send up to 6 photos; we make a short reel for your business from them." Button: Subscribe on WhatsApp.
2. **How a reel is made** (3 steps):
   1. وصفك: ماذا تريد أن يعرف الناس؟ / Your description: what should people know?
   2. صورك: حتى 6 صور لمشروعك أو منتجاتك. / Your photos: up to 6 of your business or products.
   3. الموافقة: ترى الريل وتوافق عليه أو تطلب تعديلًا قبل التسليم. / Approval: you see the reel and approve it or ask for a
      change before it is delivered.
3. **In your brand look.** Colours and fonts from the brand look set up in month 1.
4. **Published for you.** «بعد موافقتك، ينشره شخص من فريقنا على صفحاتك ويرد على التعليقات والرسائل.» / "Once you approve, a person on
   our team publishes it on your pages and replies to comments and messages."
5. **Part of every package.** Reels come with posts, stories and the monthly content calendar; the number of reels per package
   is on /pricing/.
6. **Short FAQ:** what photos work best (clear photos of the place, products or team); which languages (Arabic, English or both);
   can I change a reel (yes, before you approve; revision rounds per package on /pricing/).
7. **Close.** Subscribe on WhatsApp / create your account.
