Clear answers

How does payment work?

Message us on WhatsApp and we send you our InstaPay details. You pay by InstaPay and send us the transfer receipt in the same chat. You can create your account and tell us about your business before you pay. Nothing is charged until you agree, and work starts once payment is received. Every package is billed monthly in Egyptian pounds, and the pricing page lists what each one includes.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel before your next renewal in your account, or by emailing team@crierstudio.com. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you have already paid for, and your package stays active until then.

Can I pay once, with no subscription?

No. Every package is monthly. You can cancel before your next renewal, and your package stays active until the end of the paid month.

What is coming to In-store?

A set of items your shop uses every day, such as aprons, menu boards, door mats, cups, QR stands and stamp cards, designed for your business in your own identity. It is not open yet. The In-store page shows the kind of items a café, a restaurant or a salon could get.

When does In-store open?

There is no launch date yet. Join the waitlist and we will email you when it opens.

Can I order custom pieces now?

Yes. Use the Ask for a quote on WhatsApp button on the In-store page, and tell us what you want made, where it will be used and when you need it.

What is the Brand Deck?

The Brand Deck is coming soon. Orders and payments are not open yet. It is one deck that shows your business, your colours and your products: an identity page, one page per product and a catalogue, as a screen PDF and a print-ready PDF.

Will you change my logo?

No. The Brand Deck is not a logo redesign. We use your current logo as it is.

What files do I get?

A screen PDF (A4 landscape, RGB) and a print-ready PDF (CMYK, 3 mm bleed, crop marks). They are digital files only, and nothing is shipped.

How much will the Brand Deck cost?

The Brand Deck is coming soon. Orders and payments are not open yet.

How long does it take?

We do not promise a delivery time in advance. We confirm the timing by email when we reply.

Can you print it?

We give you a print-ready PDF. Printing is not included, and nothing is shipped.

What do you need from me?

Create your account and tell us about your business. Before we start, and before you pay, we need: your logo, at least one of your Instagram, Facebook or TikTok accounts with its username, your goal, who your customers are, and your country. We also ask what you offer, your city, your current offers and the style you like.

For the posts themselves we need real photos. For a shop, a café or another place people visit: 10 photos of your business, or 6 of your products, each with a photo and its price. For an app, a website or a service: at least 4 screens of your own app or website; we capture them from your site, or you upload a few. Each month we also ask for last month's numbers, if you have them. We never put stock photos of strangers in your posts.

Which languages do you work in?

This site is in English and Arabic, and you can write to us in either. Your posts and captions can be in Arabic, English or both. The Brand Deck can be made in English, Arabic or both.

Who publishes the posts?

We do. Once you approve a post, we publish it on your pages on its date and reply to comments and messages. Nothing is published before you approve it.

Who owns the designs you make for me?

The designs, captions and Brand Deck files we deliver to you are yours to use in your business. Your logo, name and brand stay yours, and we do not show work made for you in public without your written approval.

Can you promise more customers?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that. We deliver the designs and the content calendar described on the pricing page. Results depend on your business, your market and how you use them.

What can I do in my account?

Your account is your own private page at portal.crierstudio.com. We put your posts there, and you review each one, ask for changes and approve it. You can create a free account there yourself.

What happens to my details?

We use them to prepare what you asked for and to contact you about it. You can ask us to export or delete them at any time at team@crierstudio.com. See the privacy policy for details.
